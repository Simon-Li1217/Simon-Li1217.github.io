DIFF-TRO reformulates IoT topology robustness optimization as a denoising diffusion process over spatio-temporal graph embeddings. It learns robustness-oriented structural priors directly from unlabeled topologies—mitigating both the path dependence of search-based heuristics and the label scarcity of supervised methods—and uses a constraint-aware decoding strategy (degree balancing + component bridging) together with a closed-loop self-supervised training mechanism to generate geometrically feasible and highly robust networks. Extensive experiments across diverse network scales and densities show consistent improvements over heuristic and reinforcement-learning baselines.
