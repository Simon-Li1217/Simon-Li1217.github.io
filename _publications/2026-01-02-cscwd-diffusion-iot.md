This work proposes a diffusion-based self-supervised framework for IoT topology robustness optimization. A spatio-temporal topology embedding with a noise-injection diffusion strategy learns robustness-related structural priors from unlabeled topologies, while a constraint-aware edge-attribute decoding strategy incorporates communication range, degree balance, and component-bridging constraints to transform the diffusion-generated confidence matrix into feasible, connected, and robust networks. Experiments across varying densities and scales demonstrate state-of-the-art robustness, fast convergence, and competitive runtime versus evolutionary and RL baselines.
